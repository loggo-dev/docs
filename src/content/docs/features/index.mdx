Loggo is a highly specialized self-hosted note app tailored for engineers and thinkers.

## A Day is a Board

Instead of endless scrolling feeds or nested folder structures, Loggo organizes your life by day. Every day gives you a fresh, infinite 2D canvas. You drop "Logs" onto it anywhere you want. See [Canvas and Masonry](/features/canvas-and-masonry) for the two ways to view a day.

## Plain Markdown with Frontmatter

Every Log you create is seamlessly mirrored to the filesystem as a standard `.md` file, complete with YAML frontmatter. This guarantees your data is always portable, readable, and under your absolute control. See [Portability and Rebuild](/features/portability) for how that mirror doubles as a real backup.

## Blazing Fast FTS5

Loggo embeds a SQLite database leveraging FTS5 and the `unicode61` tokenizer. Finding a single keyword across thousands of your historical logs takes just milliseconds. See [Search](/features/search) for the Cmd+K palette this powers.

## Everything else

| Feature | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| [Markdown Editor](/features/markdown-editor) | CodeMirror 6 with Obsidian-style live preview, syntax-highlighted code blocks, paste-to-upload images |
| [Tags](/features/tags) | Inline `#tag` (and `#nested/tag`) parsed out of a Log's body automatically |
| [Tasks](/features/tasks) | `- [ ]` checkboxes parsed into real records, with `!friday`-style due dates |
| [Attachments](/features/attachments) | Images and files attached to a Log, stored locally, on S3, or on R2 |
| [Workspaces](/features/workspaces) | A private Personal space plus shared team workspaces, each with its own color and icon |
| [Daily Templates](/features/templates) | Logs a workspace generates automatically for today, or for any day you open |
| [Admin](/features/admin) | Managing users, workspaces, and instance-wide settings |
