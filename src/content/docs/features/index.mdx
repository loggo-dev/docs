Loggo is a highly specialized self-hosted note app tailored for engineers and thinkers.

## A Day is a Board

Instead of endless scrolling feeds or nested folder structures, Loggo organizes your life by day. Every day gives you a fresh, infinite 2D canvas. You drop "Logs" onto it anywhere you want.

## Plain Markdown with Frontmatter

Every Log you create is seamlessly mirrored to the filesystem as a standard `.md` file, complete with YAML frontmatter. This guarantees your data is always portable, readable, and under your absolute control.

## Blazing Fast FTS5

Loggo embeds a SQLite database leveraging FTS5 and the `unicode61` tokenizer. Finding a single keyword across thousands of your historical logs takes just milliseconds.
