<Steps>
  <Step title="Open today's board">
    Every day in Loggo gets its own dedicated board.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Drop Logs anywhere">
    In Canvas mode, drag a Log wherever you like across an infinite 2D
    space, panning and zooming with your mouse or trackpad.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Or switch to Masonry">
    Prefer structure? Masonry mode packs the same Logs into a clean,
    responsive grid instead.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Infinite Canvas

In **Canvas mode**, you have an infinite 2D space. You can drag your logs anywhere you like, pan around using your mouse or trackpad, and zoom in and out. This is perfect for mind-mapping or keeping a chaotic scratchpad of your day.

## Masonry Grid

If you prefer a structured view, **Masonry mode** automatically packs all your logs into a clean, responsive grid layout.
