Every day in Loggo gives you a dedicated board. You can choose how you view it:

## Infinite Canvas
In **Canvas mode**, you have an infinite 2D space. You can drag your logs anywhere you like, pan around using your mouse or trackpad, and zoom in and out. This is perfect for mind-mapping or keeping a chaotic scratchpad of your day.

## Masonry Grid
If you prefer a structured view, **Masonry mode** automatically packs all your logs into a clean, responsive grid layout.
